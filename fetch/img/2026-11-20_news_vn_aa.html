Azerbaijan president begins 3-day visit to Vietnam

Ilham Aliyev lands at Noi Bai International Airport in Hanoi
Aamir Latif
19 November 2026•Update: 19 November 2026
ISLAMABAD

Azerbaijan's President Ilham Aliyev began a three-day visit Thursday to Vietnam, according to his office and Vietnamese state media.

Aliyev landed at Noi Bai International Airport in the capital, Hanoi, where he was received by Justice Minister Hoang Thanh Tung and other officials, the Vietnam News Agency reported.

A guard of honor was presented to the president soon after his arrival.

Aliyev is visiting Hanoi at the invitation of General Secretary of the Communist Party of Vietnam and State President To Lam.

It is Aliyev’s second official visit to Vietnam, following his first in 2014. It is expected to help usher Vietnam-Azerbaijan relations into a new phase of development under a Strategic Partnership framework.

To Lam’s state visit to Azerbaijan in May 2025 and the two countries’ formal elevation of ties to a Strategic Partnership opened a new chapter in relations.

The visit is expected to expand cooperation across a wide range of areas, including politics, diplomacy, economy, trade, investment, energy, education, science and technology, and people-to-people exchanges.

Azerbaijan and Vietnam established relations in 1992.
