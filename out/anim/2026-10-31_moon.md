## How it was made
- three.js r160 in headless Chromium (Playwright's bundled headless shell; the brief's `-1194` path no longer exists
  in this container, the bundled `-1243` one was used) with SwiftShader. Earth = custom shader (day map, normal map,
  specular ocean glint, city lights on the night side, soft terminator with warm twilight, moving clouds + cloud
  shadows, limb haze) + ray-traced atmosphere shell; Moon = standard material with bump; 9 000 point stars +
  procedural Milky Way; UnrealBloom, vignette, light grain. Text is DOM over the canvas, so it is pin-sharp at 1080p.
- Camera: two shots (hook push, then a dip-to-black cut into one continuous Catmull-Rom move); framings solved
  in code ("put Earth at pixel X and the Moon at pixel Y") so nothing important leaves the frame.
- Process: ~7 rounds of stills + one 10-fps motion preview, then the full 1080p render.
- HyperFrames: cloned and read the README; NOT used as the renderer — it drives headless Chrome frame by frame
  exactly like our render.js, and our scene already has a deterministic `renderAt(t)`; given SwiftShader WebGL I
  kept the known-good loop instead of spending the 20 minutes on its CLI install. Lemo-Opuscar: read DIRECTOR.md +
  satellite-briefing STYLE.md and applied its rules (counters roll and land with a pop, every action has a sound,
  one "breath" of near-silence before the turn, −14 LUFS, labels never cover the subject, no blank transition frames).
- Closest reference: **story.gaffer** (3D data story: dark space, one 3D metaphor per beat, top-left white headline
  with one yellow keyword, small mono HUD tags, counter/dial instruments). Progress bar 1/3 idea from advice.digital.
