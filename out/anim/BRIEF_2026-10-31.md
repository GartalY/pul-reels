# Night animation test — brief (Gartal asked 2026-10-28)

Make ONE high-quality animated vertical reel (1080x1920, 20–35 s), entirely code-generated 3D
(three.js rendered in headless Chromium like prototypes/cine; executablePath
/opt/pw-browsers/chromium_headless_shell-1194/chrome-linux/headless_shell with SwiftShader flags;
`npm install three@0.160.0 playwright` in the scene folder). Budget: take your time — up to ~3 hours (Gartal OK'd 2–3 h per piece at night) (Gartal: "even if it takes 2 hours, take your time"; he wants it to look like the best animated reels on Instagram).

Quality bar: a polished short film, not a prototype — smooth camera, good lighting, soft shadows/fog,
believable space materials, no flicker, no jitter.

Topic: "Ay hər il Yerdən təxminən 3,8 sm uzaqlaşır" — the Moon drifts ~3.8 cm/year away; tidal braking
lengthens Earth's day; ~1.4 billion years ago a day was ~18 hours.
Visuals: Earth and Moon in space with realistic shading, Moon slowly receding along its orbit, a clean
distance counter, a day-length clock going 18 h → 24 h.

Text/voice: 3 natural Azerbaijani beats (RUNBOOK language + hook rules: number-first hook, complete natural
sentences, numbers spoken as words in the voice text), narrated with voice.py (Banu, +15%), music from
reelgen.music(), hook + beats + follow CTA in the SAFE ZONE (x 70–890, y ≤ 1450, dark backing), factgen
fonts/colors. Check ≥ 8 frames visually; fix anything ugly.

Output: out/anim/2026-10-31_moon.mp4 (ffmpeg crf 23, < 45 MB), scene code in prototypes/anim_moon/,
notes in out/anim/2026-10-31_moon.md (topic, beats, sources, render time, what went well/badly).
DO NOT schedule or publish anything — Gartal reviews it first (sent to him at 09:45 by a separate task).

Assets ready in the repo: assets/textures/ (earth_atmos_2048, earth_normal_2048, earth_specular_2048,
earth_clouds_1024, earth_lights_2048, moon_1024 — NASA-based, from three.js examples). Use a starfield,
atmosphere glow (fresnel shader), day/night terminator with city lights, slowly rotating clouds, cinematic
camera moves (slow dolly/orbit with easing), depth of field / bloom via three.js postprocessing if it renders
reliably in SwiftShader; render at 1080x1920 directly if speed allows, else 720x1280 upscaled.

Poly Haven (CC0 HDRIs, textures, models) is reachable: polyhaven.com and dl.polyhaven.org work (direct file URLs like
https://dl.polyhaven.org/file/ph-assets/HDRIs/hdr/1k/<name>_1k.hdr). Use them for lighting/materials where useful.

  the render engine instead of our hand-rolled Playwright loop if it works headless here. If it fails in 20 min,
  fall back to prototypes/cine/render.js.

  the chosen style's folder. For the Moon topic try satellite-briefing or dark-keynote; keep our safe zone and
  Azerbaijani text rules (natural sentences, İ/ə correct).
