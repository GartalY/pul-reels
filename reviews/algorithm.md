# Algorithm watch — Instagram / TikTok / YouTube Shorts (@bilirdinizmi_az)

Weekly check (requested by Gartal 2026-10-27): what changed in ranking and creator best practice in the last 7–14 days.
Official sources first, then reputable industry coverage. Every claim dated and sourced; undated "hacks" ignored.

## 2026-11-01 (covers ~18 Oct – 1 Nov)

### Instagram
- **CONFIRMED — "Features": long-form video up to 90 minutes, test only** (26 Oct 2026; Instagram press release via
  [TechCrunch, A. Silberling, 26 Oct](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/26/watch-out-youtube-instagram-goes-from-short-form-to-90-minute-videos/)
  and [Social Media Today, A. Hutchinson, 26 Oct](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-rolls-out-limited-features-test-in-the-us/872825/)).
  Tested in Brazil, now select US creators. "Clips as Reels" lets a long video link up to 8 Reels; a "Series" topic tag for Reels is in development.
  Mosseri (Threads, 27 Oct, quoted by the [Keywords Everywhere tracker](https://keywordseverywhere.com/news/instagram-algorithm-updates/)):
  "Features is not fully launched yet, we're testing and ramping up access slowly."
- **CONFIRMED (earnings call, not a product post) — Reels ranking looks at sequences + freshness** (Meta Q3 2026 call, 21 Oct, CFO Susan Li,
  via the Keywords Everywhere tracker): ranking now handles sequences of Reels "at a time instead of evaluating each Reel independently";
  relevance launches raised Reels time spent "by over 4 %"; "nearly 45 %" of recommended Reels are under a day old. Internal metrics, no dates/markets.
- RUMOUR / analysis only: that Features is aimed at YouTube creators (TechCrunch's view; PetaPixel 30 Oct reports payment offers to YouTube creators — not checked against an official source).

### YouTube Shorts
- **CONFIRMED — Shorts recommendations favour original content** (YouTube statement reported by
  [Social Media Today, A. Hutchinson, 1 Oct 2026](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/youtube-updates-shorts-algorithm-to-put-more-focus-on-original-content/831976/);
  just outside the 14-day window, listed because we only started Shorts on 27 Oct). Channels that mainly re-upload other creators' videos
  without meaningful changes "will likely see less distribution within the Shorts feed". No size of the cut given.

### TikTok
- **No confirmed ranking change found in the last 14 days.** TikTok newsroom items were creator programmes / ad tools (e.g. Tubi × TikTok
  "Creatorverse" cohort, 27 Oct). Search results promising a "new TikTok algorithm Oct 2026" were undated creator videos — ignored.

### What it means for a small Azerbaijani facts page
- Features / long-form on Instagram: not available to us (US/Brazil test). Nothing to do; our long videos stay on YouTube.
- Freshness (45 % of recommended Reels < 1 day old) **matches our data**: reels stop growing after ~3 days (Boulevard +3 views in a day at 75 h),
  and the same-day trend reel is our 2nd-best IG slot (18:30 median 171 vs 146 at 21:30 and 54 at 16:00). Keep the daily trend reel; no change needed.
- YouTube originality: our videos are our own, so reposting them to YouTube is fine. But a DUPLICATE of the same video on our channel
  looks like a re-upload — and our only duplicate (Osimhen, 27 Oct) got 9 views vs median 799. **Applied to RUNBOOK:** one upload per video per channel.
