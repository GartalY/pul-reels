# Ep01 — What if every bee disappeared? — research checked 2026-11-02 (re-verify anything marked VERIFY)

- More than 75% of the world's food crop TYPES rely at least partly on pollination; crops directly
  affected by pollinators are worth US$235–577 billion a year; ~20,000 wild bee species; more than 40% of
  invertebrate pollinator species (mostly bees, butterflies) face extinction in regional assessments;
  pollinator-dependent production volume up 300% in 50 years.
  Source: IPBES pollination assessment press release (2016) https://ipbes.net/article/press-release-pollinators-vital-our-food-supply-under-threat
- By VOLUME it's different: pollinator-dependent crops ≈ 35% of global food production; the big staples
  (wheat, rice, maize) are wind-pollinated and would survive. Klein et al. 2007, Proc R Soc B. VERIFY wording.
- Complete loss of animal pollinators: +71 million people vitamin-A deficient, +173 million folate
  deficient, ~1.42 million extra deaths a year (+2.7% of all deaths). Smith et al. 2015, The Lancet.
  https://news.harvard.edu/gazette/story/2015/07/quality-and-quantity-of-key-crops-changing
- Already today, inadequate pollination is linked to ~427,000 excess deaths a year (Smith et al. 2022,
  Environmental Health Perspectives). VERIFY the number. https://hsph.harvard.edu/news/pollination-loss-removes-healthy-foods-from-global-diets-increases-chronic-diseases-causing-excess-deaths/
- California almonds: ~2.7 million honeybee colonies needed for the 2024 bloom (~1.4 million acres) —
  about 99% of all US colonies counted on 1 Jan 2024; fees average over $200 per colony.
  farmdoc daily, Feb 2025 https://farmdocdaily.illinois.edu/2025/02/where-have-all-the-honey-bees-gone-to-california-almond-orchards.html
- Maoxian County, Sichuan (China): people hand-pollinate apple and pear trees with brushes/sticks because
  wild pollinators collapsed (pesticides, habitat). Mountain Research and Development 32(2), 2012,
  "The Human Pollinators of Fruit Crops in Maoxian County". VERIFY: since when, trees/day, tools, cost.
- The "Einstein: 4 years without bees" quote is fake. Earliest known: May 1965, French magazine
  "La Vie des Bêtes"; no evidence Einstein said it. https://quoteinvestigator.com/2013/08/27/einstein-bees/
  → great cold-open myth-bust.
- CHOCOLATE IS NOT BEE-POLLINATED: cacao is pollinated by tiny biting midges (Forcipomyia, 1–3 mm);
  roughly 1 in 400–500 flowers becomes a pod. NHM https://www.nhm.ac.uk/discover/flies-are-saving-your-chocolate-cravings.html
  (Claude's Kokoro voice test line said chocolate needs bees — that was WRONG, don't repeat it.)
- Important nuance for honesty: "every bee" ≠ "every pollinator" — flies, beetles, butterflies, birds,
  bats and wind would still pollinate. Don't claim humanity dies; claim diets get poorer, prices rise,
  many fruits/nuts/vegetables become luxuries.
