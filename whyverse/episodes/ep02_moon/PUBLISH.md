**Title:** What If the Moon Disappeared? (It's Slowly Leaving)

**Description:**
If the Moon vanished right now, you wouldn't notice for 1.3 seconds. After that, the night switches off, and a lot more changes than you'd think: the tides, a reef that times its spawning by the Moon, the length of our days, maybe even our seasons.
