**Title:** Why Is the Sky Dark at Night? (It Should Be Blazing)

**Description:**
If space went on forever and was full of stars, every direction you look would end on a star, and the night sky would blaze like the surface of the Sun. It doesn't. The reason is one of the best clues we have that the universe had a beginning.
