# Ep07 — How much is the whole world actually worth? — RESEARCH (checked 13 Nov 2026)

Every number in the script, with its source. Second sources where found. "Narration" = how the script says it.

1. **What people own ≈ $518 trillion (end of 2025)** — UBS Global Wealth Report 2026 (30 Jun 2026). UBS gives no single headline total;
   adding its wealth-pyramid bands (p.23: 3.22 tn + 63.16 tn + 200.72 tn + 250.59 tn) gives ≈ $517.7 tn for "56 markets we estimate to represent
   over 92% of the world's wealth". Narration: "Add up the figures in its latest report … about five hundred and eighteen trillion … only the fifty-six markets it tracks."
   PDF https://www.ubs.com/content/dam/assets/wm/static/gwr/global-wealth-report-en-2026.pdf · press release https://www.ubs.com/global/en/media/display-page-ndp/en-20260630-gwr-2026.html
2. **+10.8 % in 2025 (USD)** — UBS GWR 2026 p.8 "Total personal wealth grew by 10.8% when measured in USD"; 2nd: InvestmentNews
   https://www.investmentnews.com/practice-management/global-wealth-grew-at-fastest-pace-in-eight-years-during-2025-ubs-report-finds/267240 → "almost eleven percent".
3. **Nearly one million new USD millionaires in 2025** — UBS press release: "equivalent to nearly one million new millionaires globally".
4. **≈ $130,000 per adult if shared evenly** — our own division: $517.7 tn ÷ ≈ 3.85 bn adults in UBS's pyramid bands (1.62 bn + 1.58 bn + 588 m + 58 m) ≈ $134,600.
   UBS states no global average/median; said as "shared out evenly … about a hundred and thirty thousand dollars for every adult in those markets".
5. **Counting $1 a second → ~16 million years** — arithmetic: 5.18 × 10¹⁴ s ÷ 3.156 × 10⁷ s/yr ≈ 16.4 million years. Homo sapiens ≈ 300,000 years
   (Smithsonian Human Origins, widely cited) shown as a sliver.
6. **Real estate $393.3 tn; residential $286.9 tn (≈ 73 %); commercial $58.5 tn; agricultural land $47.9 tn** — Savills, 29 Sep 2025 ("as of the start of 2025");
   "the world's largest store of wealth"; worth more than "the combined value of global equities, debt, and gold".
   https://www.savills.us/insight-and-opinion/savills-news/381209/world-s-real-estate-worth-393-3-trillion-and-is-the-world-s-largest-store-of-wealth ·
   https://impacts.savills.com/market-trends/how-much-is-global-real-estate-worth.html
7. **All gold ever mined: 224,200 t (end Q3 2026); cube ≈ 22.6 m a side** — World Gold Council above-ground stocks (page dated 3 Nov 2026): "224,200 tonnes, US$30.0tn";
   "the resulting cube of pure gold would only measure around 22.6 metres on each side". Check: 224,200 t ÷ 19.32 g/cm³ = 11,605 m³ → cube root 22.64 m.
   Breakdown: jewellery ≈ 44 %, bars + coins ≈ 21 %, central banks ≈ 18 %, other (ETFs, OTC, other) ≈ 17 %.
   https://www.gold.org/goldhub/data/above-ground-stocks · https://www.gold.org/about-gold/gold-supply/gold-mining/how-much-gold-has-been-mined
   A football pitch is ~105 × 68 m, so the cube fits with room around it. Gold density 19.32 g/cm³ (→ "19.3 × heavier than water").
8. **Gold ≈ $3,900/oz → ≈ $28 tn** — spot $3,890.88 (USAGOLD, 13 Nov 2026 https://www.usagold.com/daily-gold-market-report/); $3,874.65 (Trading Economics 13 Nov 2026
   https://tradingeconomics.com/commodity/gold). 224,200 t × 32,150.7 oz/t = 7.21 bn oz × $3,880 ≈ $28.0 tn (WGC's $30.0 tn used the higher end-Sept price).
   $28 tn ÷ $518 tn ≈ 5.4 % → "about one twentieth".
9. **US money: M2 $23.4 tn, currency $2.40 tn (Sept 2026, SA) → ≈ 1 in 10** — Federal Reserve H.6 released 27 Oct 2026 https://www.federalreserve.gov/RELEASES/h6/Current/ ;
   cross-check H.6 22 Sep 2026 (Aug: currency $2,389.7 bn, M2 $23,342.8 bn) https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/h6/20260922/ ; FRED M2NS.
   2,397 / 23,414 = 10.2 %. (No solid global cash-share figure exists — not used.)
10. **Banks lend deposits out; loans create deposits** — standard central-bank explanation (e.g. Bank of England, "Money creation in the modern economy", Quarterly Bulletin 2014 Q1). Said qualitatively, no number.
11. **Nature's services ≈ $125 tn per year (2011) vs world GDP ≈ $75 tn; loss from land-use change $4.3–20.2 tn/yr** — Costanza et al. 2014,
    "Changes in the global value of ecosystem services", Global Environmental Change 26: 152–158, DOI 10.1016/j.gloenvcha.2014.04.002 (values in 2007 US$; $125 tn with
    updated unit values and biome areas). Author PDF https://www.robertcostanza.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/02/2014_J_Costanza_GlobalValueUpdate.pdf ·
    UCL https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/10189453/ . Authors: an accounting estimate, not a market price (said in c4_7).
12. **World Bank: total wealth $1,152 tn (2018), 13 × world GDP (~$86 tn); human capital 64 %** — The Changing Wealth of Nations 2021.
    Press release 27 Oct 2021 ("human capital (earnings over a person's lifetime) … 64% of total global wealth in 2018"; "the population's expected lifetime earnings")
    https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2021/10/27/global-wealth-has-grown-but-at-the-expense-of-future-prosperity-world-bank ;
    Tokyo presentation slide "Human capital 64%" https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/178280692476835742b62ff8a4cf5a9e-0360012021/original/The-Changing-Wealth-of-Nations-2021-Presentation-for-Tokyo.pdf ;
    $1,152 tn / 13× via Conversable Economist quoting the report https://conversableeconomist.com/2022/04/07/world-wealth-human-physical-and-natural/ .
    Produced 31 % / natural 6 % NOT verified → shown only as "everything else ≈ 36 %".
13. **Earth's mass 5.9722 × 10²⁴ kg; diameter 12,742 km** — NASA Earth Fact Sheet https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/earthfact.html
14. **Bulk Earth ≈ 32.0 % iron by mass, mostly in the core** — McDonough (2003), Compositional Model for the Earth's Core, Treatise on Geochemistry
    (tabulated https://www.knowledgedoor.com/2/elements_handbook/element_abundances_in_the_bulk_earth.html); Anderson, Theory of the Earth (core 30–34 wt %).
15. **Iron ore ≈ $90/t** — 62 % Fe CFR $90.3/t on 27 Oct 2026 (Kallanish via GMK Center, 29 Oct 2026 https://gmk.center/?p=130909); Dalian near 2-year lows on 13 Nov (Trading Economics).
16. **The scrapyard sum** — 0.32 × 5.9722e24 kg = 1.91 × 10²¹ t × $90 = 1.72 × 10²³ $ ≈ 170 sextillion; ÷ $518 tn ≈ 332 million → "over three hundred million times".
    Deliberately a joke figure (ore is ~62 % iron; the core can't be mined) — the script says so ("Of course, that's nonsense").
17. **Greg Laughlin's ≈ $5 quadrillion (Feb 2011)** — tongue-in-cheek formula for pricing Kepler exoplanets (mass, temperature, age/type/brightness of the star). Original: Boing Boing
    interview by Lee Billings, 3 Feb 2011; confirmed by Kottke 4 Feb 2011 https://kottke.org/11/02/how-much-is-a-planet-worth and Donald Marron
    https://dmarron.com/2011/02/04/i-bid-28000-for-mars/ ("Earth is worth about $5 quadrillion").

## Deliberately NOT said
- A UBS "global average/median wealth" or UBS "total world wealth" as UBS's own headline (we summed its bands; 56 markets only).
- Gold stocks of 216,265 t / 219,891 t (outdated); Costanza in "2011 dollars" (it's 2007 US$); iron ore "$100/t".
- A global share of money that is cash (no solid source) — only the US figure.
- World Bank produced/natural split (unverified); "real estate = 3 × equities" (dates don't match WFE end-2025 $152 tn).
- Wealth inequality numbers (top 1 % shares etc.) — only the neutral statement that it isn't shared evenly.
