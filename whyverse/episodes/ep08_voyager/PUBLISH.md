**Title:** Voyager 1 Is One Light-Day Away. How Do We Still Hear It?

**Description:**
On 18 November 2026, Voyager 1 becomes the first human-made object one light-day from Earth: a hello sent to it takes a whole day to arrive.
Its radio is about as strong as a fridge bulb and its computers have less memory than one phone photo — so how do we still hear it after 49 years?
