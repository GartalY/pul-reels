**Description:**
A banana fires a speck of antimatter roughly every 75 minutes, and it vanishes in a flash of light. Our best theory says the Big Bang made matter and antimatter in equal amounts, so the universe should be empty.
So why does anything exist? Antimatter, the traps that hold it, and the one-in-a-billion clue.
