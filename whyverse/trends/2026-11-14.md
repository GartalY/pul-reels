## 3. Crows decide in 235 milliseconds — about twice as fast as humans — political: false
**Hook:** "235 milliseconds. That's how fast a crow makes up its mind."

**Why hot now:** published in Science, ScienceDaily feature 12 Nov, Tübingen press release; circulating as a
"crows are smarter than you think" story. Lighter freshness — used for today's 23:00 static fact reel (YouTube).

Facts:
- Carrion crows (Corvus corone) judged whether a cloud of moving dots went left or right and pecked the matching
  side, on average in 235 ms — ScienceDaily 12 Nov (https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/11/261111005850.htm)
- Neurons in the nidopallium caudolaterale told the two directions apart 70 ms after the dots appeared — same.
- University of Tübingen: about twice as fast as humans and monkeys on this kind of task — Tübingen press release
  (https://uni-tuebingen.de/en/university/news-and-publications/press-release/crows-make-decisions-twice-as-fast-as-humans-and-monkeys/)
- Accuracy nearly 100% with clear motion — ScienceDaily.
- Schmidbauer & Nieder, Science 394 (6822): 445, 2026, doi 10.1126/science.aed6459.

Avoid: "crows are smarter than humans"; a human ms figure (not given); the sample is two birds — say "in a lab test".
